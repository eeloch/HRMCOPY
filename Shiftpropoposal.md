The shift system at Rotic is designed to ensure that production operations continue efficiently while employees rotate between Day and Night Shifts.

Day Shift
The regular day shift runs from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Employees assigned to the day shift generally report to work in the morning and work throughout the production day.

Day Shift Schedule:
Start Time: 7:00 AM
Closing Time: 7:00 PM
Regular Work Period: Monday to Saturday

During this period, employees assigned to the day shift are expected to resume at their respective workstations and handover properly at the end of their shift. Handover time is 6:50 AM.

Night Shift
The night shift runs from 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM the following morning.

Employees on the night shift take over production activities from the day-shift team to ensure continuity of operations.

Night Shift Schedule:
Start Time: 7:00 PM
Closing Time: 7:00 AM the following morning

Sunday Shift:

Night work only. 
Shift Schedule:
Start time: 7:00 PM
Closing Time: 7:00 AM (Monday morning) this is for shift change. this work starts with workers that worked the dy shift from previous week 

The night shift team must ensure that all necessary information, production status, equipment conditions, outstanding issues and safety concerns are  properly communicated during handover. Handover time 6:50 PM






Shift Rotation
The transition between the day and night shift occurs during a weekly rotation period. Sunday night / Sunday morning changeover.


Scenario:
If an employee has been working Day Shift:
Monday - Saturday 
7:00 AM - 7:00 PM

When the rotation changes, the employee will move to the night-shift schedule:
Sunday - Sunday
7:00 PM - 7:00 AM 

Permanent Shift Employes
Not every employee necessarily follows the same rotation system,
Some employees are assigned to a permanent day or permanent night shift, depending on their department.
Nb. Some Administrative Staff work from 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM and 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM
